## Outcome

**Combined real-data verdict summary** (all real BTC/USDT data, `tenant_id=271d391dd7bf4213b3e5fb8ea6636563`,
`binance_price_btcusdt_1h`, bounded window `2026-01-10T00:00:00`–`2026-08-07T09:00:00`, 5,026 raw rows /
5,025 returns, `train_window=500, test_window=50, step=250`, 18 splits per horizon):

| Horizon | Model | better | worse | no significant difference | purge_gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h | LightGBMBaseline | 0 | 4 | 14 | 6 |
| 1h | RegimeHMMBaseline | 0 | 0 | 18 | 6 |
| 6h | LightGBMBaseline | 0 | 2 | 16 | 6 |
| 6h | RegimeHMMBaseline | 0 | 0 | 18 | 6 |
| 24h | LightGBMBaseline | 0 | 2 | 16 | 24 (Harvey-corrected) |
| 24h | RegimeHMMBaseline | 0 | 0 | 18 | 24 (Harvey-corrected) |

Neither candidate model beat Naive0 in a stable, statistically significant way at any of the three
horizons on real, recent BTC/USDT data — a second, independent confirmation (real data, not just synthetic)
of this platform's core finding, on model classes (gradient boosting, regime-switching) the thesis itself
had flagged as untested. `RegimeHMMBaseline` shows zero "better" or "worse" verdicts across all three
horizons (uniformly "no significant difference"); `LightGBMBaseline` shows a small number of "worse"
verdicts and zero "better" verdicts at every horizon — if anything, mildly underperforming Naive0 on this
real window, never beating it.

**Hard gate**: not triggered — no real, Harvey-corrected, statistically significant DM win over Naive0 was
produced at any horizon, so no product-copy/positioning change is authorized by this sprint's results, and
none was made.
