## Explicit purge-gap decision for 24h (binding, made here rather than left to the dev agent)

`libs/naive_first_engine`'s own regression suite already answers this question for the thesis's original
protocol: `test_regression_1h.py`, `test_regression_6h.py`, and `test_regression_24h.py` **all** use
`_PURGE_GAP = 24` (`da-tese-ao-produto.md` section 1.2's own thesis default), applied uniformly regardless
of horizon — not `purge_gap = horizon`. MR-004/MR-005's synthetic-data tickets used a smaller `purge_gap=6`
(the max of their own 1h/6h horizons) as a light-compute-scoping choice specific to their small synthetic
dataset, not a claim that 6 is sufficient in general.

**Decision**: this ticket uses `purge_gap=24` for its horizon=24 run — wider than MR-008's `purge_gap=6`,
matching both the thesis's own published-numbers convention and the minimum requirement to prevent a
24-hours-ahead target window from overlapping into the very next split's training rows. This is the
"legitimate methodological question" the backlog's own AC anticipates, decided explicitly here (not
silently defaulted to MR-008's `purge_gap=6`, which would under-purge a 24h-ahead target).
